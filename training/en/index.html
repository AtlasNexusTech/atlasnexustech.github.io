The gap

Conversational AI ≠ Agentic AI : two worlds

Conversational AI answers. An agent acts. That is the difference between an advisor and a teammate.

Conversational AI : today's level

A conversational brain, without hands

  • ✕ Generates text, code, ideas, on request
  • ✕ Does nothing by itself : it waits for your prompt
  • ✕ No access to your tools, files or systems
  • ✕ No durable working memory between tasks
  • ✕ Quality depends on whoever asks the questions

The LLM you use : ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini are LLMs (Large Language Models), the engine behind conversational AI : a model trained on massive volumes of text that generates language by predicting what comes next.

Agentic AI : the next level

A teammate who takes initiative

  • ✓ Receives a goal, not an instruction
  • ✓ Plans, executes and iterates until the result
  • ✓ Uses tools: web, files, APIs, software
  • ✓ Remembers its context and reports back
  • ✓ Works alone, in the background, while you supervise

What is an agentic AI? An agentic AI : an "agent" : is an AI that acts : it receives a goal, plans, uses tools (web, files, APIs, software) and works autonomously until the result, while reporting back. Where conversational AI waits for your questions, the agent takes initiative under your supervision.

The gap goes far beyond a technical tool : it is about embracing a paradigm shift for your business, through structured coaching tailored to your stakes.

The structural change

What really changes, for individuals and businesses

For individuals

Your value will no longer be what you produce, but what you orchestrate

  • → The conversational-AI prompter stays a user. The agent operator becomes a multiplier.
  • → A "sales + AI agents" or "ops + AI agents" profile is worth twice as much.
  • → New roles : agent designer, workflow supervisor, orchestration engineer.
  • → The key skill: describe the outcome, not execute the task.
  • → Those who cross the gap won't be replaced by AI : they'll work with it, in tandem.

For businesses

From the individual tool to the organization that automates

  • → Conversational AI in a chat = individual gains. Agents in your processes = structural gains.
  • → Automation moves from the document to the end-to-end flow: quotes, follow-up, reminders, reporting.
  • → Teams refocus on supervision, client relationships and strategy.
  • → Competition will no longer be about headcount, but about orchestration capacity.
  • → Marginal cost of a repetitive operation → near zero, available 24/7.
